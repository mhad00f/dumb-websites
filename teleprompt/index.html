Write it in beats. A beat is one thought you can say without looking back at the screen — usually a sentence or two, up to about 25 words, which is roughly ten seconds of talking. Blank line between them. Short beats keep your eyes up where the lens is; long ones pull you back to the screen and get shrunk to fit. Anything over 60 words gets flagged before you start.
Set this device's Auto-Lock to Never before you record — the browser can't hold the screen awake over a plain-http LAN address. A Bluetooth page-turner pedal works as-is: they send arrow, space or page keys, which are the advance keys.
